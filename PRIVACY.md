# Privacy policy

Crypto & Bitcoin Analyst — Charts & Research

Effective date: 4 October 2026

## How this plugin works

This plugin contains instructions, Markdown commands and reference files that run within your Claude session. It does not include an author-operated backend, database, tracking pixel, telemetry service, account system or trade-execution service. The development validator runs only when a developer chooses to run it and has no network or analytics function.

## Conversation and research data

The plugin analyzes information you provide in Claude, such as questions, chart screenshots and project details. The author does not receive your Claude conversation through the plugin and does not collect or retain it through a plugin server. Claude handles your conversation and files under the terms, privacy policy and settings applicable to your account.

When available and permitted in your Claude session, research may use Claude's search, browsing, calculation tools or tools you have connected. Search queries and tool requests can include relevant asset names and research context and are handled by Anthropic and the chosen tool providers under their policies and your account permissions. The plugin does not add its own outbound endpoint or independently store those requests. Never provide seed phrases, private keys, login credentials or identity documents.

## Registration codes and partner offers

Registration codes are displayed only when requested. The plugin includes no registration links, embedded tracking pixels or signup forms. If you later choose to register with a code, the exchange handles registration, verification and any referral attribution under its own terms and privacy policy. The author may receive referral compensation or referral reporting from that exchange under its partner program; this is separate from your Claude conversation and is not collected by a plugin backend.

## Support

If you contact the author by email or open a GitHub issue, the author receives what you send. Support messages may be kept while resolving the issue and for follow-up; no fixed deletion schedule is promised. Ask for deletion of information in author-controlled support records at keycustodian09@gmail.com. GitHub issues are public: do not post personal financial details or credentials.

## Age and changes

The plugin is intended for adults aged 18 and over. This policy may be updated with new plugin versions. The effective date identifies the current text.
