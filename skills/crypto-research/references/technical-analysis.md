# Technical analysis reference

Use methods to answer a concrete question; do not list every available indicator. Separate observed evidence, interpretation, and a conditional future scenario.

## Inputs and calculations

- Identify pair, venue when known, timeframe, market type, snapshot time, timezone, and whether the last candle is closed.
- Inspect a supplied screenshot before discussing it. If labels, levels, indicators or volume are unreadable, say which parts cannot be assessed. Do not invent unseen panels.
- Retrieve timestamped candle data only through available tools; never claim a charting platform is connected when it is not.
- For calculations, preserve candle order, interval, and chosen settings; use enough history for the method, disclose initialization or insufficient-history limitations, and distinguish closed-candle signals from a changing live candle. Name the calculation method if variants could change the reading.
- A screenshot can support approximate price zones and visible structure. Exact indicator values require readable labels or adequate data and a reproducible calculation.

## Select the method by purpose

| Question | Relevant evidence | Interpretation limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the trend strengthening or weakening? | Swing highs/lows, SMA/EMA alignment and slope | A lagging average is supporting evidence, not a prediction. |
| Where might buyers or sellers react? | Repeated reactions, support/resistance zones, visible volume | Treat levels as zones; a touch alone does not confirm a response. |
| Is momentum changing? | RSI or MACD with stated settings | Explain momentum; avoid automatic buy/sell conclusions from thresholds or crossovers. |
| Is a breakout supported? | Close beyond a zone, volume relative to comparable periods, retest | Distinguish an intrabar move from a confirmed close and possible failed breakout. |
| Is volatility expanding? | ATR or Bollinger Bands with stated settings | Volatility alone does not establish direction. |
| Is leverage adding fragility? | Timestamped funding and open interest, available liquidation evidence | Do not infer trader direction or a certain squeeze from a single aggregate. |

## Combine evidence

Read higher-timeframe structure and the requested timeframe separately when both are available. Connect technical observations to dated fundamentals, unlocks, adoption, news and broad market conditions. Identify conflicting signals and missing evidence. Several price-derived indicators are not independent confirmations.

## Concise answer pattern

1. Direct conclusion and horizon.
2. Two or three relevant observations and their meaning.
3. Supported scenarios with trigger, invalidation and consequence; a compact table only if useful.
4. Key level/event to watch, uncertainty, dated sources or screenshot limitations, and the short risk note.

Use approximate zones when the image only supports estimates. Use numerical future targets only when their basis is explicit, conditional and supported. Never fabricate a percentage probability or describe a projection as a fact.
